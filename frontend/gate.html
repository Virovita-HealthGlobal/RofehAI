RofehAI

What this is: a working reference implementation of RofehAI's proposed architecture — a personalized, trajectory-based health-risk monitoring platform for long-duration spaceflight crews, starting with SANS (spaceflight-associated vision risk) and immune dysregulation. Includes an LLM briefing agent (single-crew and fleet-wide questions, with persistent memory) and a near-real-time clinical alert relay demonstrating detection under a simulated communication delay.

Who this is for
  • Investors and reviewers evaluating the concept and technical approach
  • Potential institutional partners (e.g. TRISH) assessing feasibility
  • Collaborators and advisors reviewing the architecture
What this is not
  • Not a clinically validated product — runs on synthetic demo data only
  • Not a medical device, not for real clinical or diagnostic use
  • Not a finished commercial product — concept / reference stage
How to use it
  • Select a crew member from the roster on the left to review their SANS and immune-dysregulation risk trajectory, confidence, and driving signals
  • Review any suggested countermeasure — the system only ever suggests; a person decides and logs the action
  • Ask the briefing agent a question about a crew member (e.g. "how is this person trending?") — it reasons across active domains and cites its sources; try a follow-up question too, it remembers the conversation
  • Use "Fleet-wide triage" in the sidebar to ask about the whole crew at once (e.g. "who needs review today?")
  • Use "Clinical alerts" to run a detection sweep, toggle the simulated comms link, and review/acknowledge any alerts — this demonstrates near-real-time clinical relay under a simulated communication delay
